Writing

Book Review: Animal Farm by George Orwell

Book review | Written as a writing assessment

Overview
Animal Farm (1945) is a short allegorical novella about the animals of Manor Farm, who overthrow their human farmer in the hope of building a fair society where all are equal. Over time, the pigs who lead the revolution slowly take control and become the very oppressors the animals had rebelled against.

Key Themes

  • Power and corruption: how those who gain power can abandon the ideals they fought for
  • Propaganda and manipulation: how language and repeated slogans are used to control a population
  • Equality versus inequality: how the promise of equality can be twisted into a new hierarchy
  • Blind trust and ignorance: how a lack of questioning allows injustice to continue
