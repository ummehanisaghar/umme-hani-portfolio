Overview
Animal Farm (1945) is an allegorical novella about a group of farm animals who overthrow their human owner in the hope of creating a fair society. As the story progresses, those who take leadership gradually reproduce the inequalities they once opposed.

Themes explored

  • Power and corruption: how authority can undermine original ideals
  • Propaganda and manipulation: how language can shape public understanding
  • Equality and hierarchy: how promises of fairness can be distorted
  • Critical thinking: why questioning information and authority matters
